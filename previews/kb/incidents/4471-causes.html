Causes to track separately

Shared parser: month-first parsing

The shared parser reads every date month-first. In a day-first tenant, a date such as 7 April (07/04) can be read as July 4. This affects app, email, and form reports.

Repository issue: Shared date parser reads day-first dates as month-first.

Outcome on 5 October: FIXED — fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. This issue is closed as completed.

booking-v3: misleading date hint

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY, including for day-first tenant locales. People follow the hint, while the form interprets the date according to the tenant locale. This cause is limited to booking-v3 form submissions.

Repository issue: booking-v3 date hint conflicts with day-first tenant locales.

Outcome on 5 October: WON'T FIX — booking-v3 form retired. This issue is closed as not_planned; retiring the form does not mean its hint was fixed.
